Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 23, 2026

1. Introduction

Halk Kompleksi ("we", "our", or "us") operates a Facebook application for automated page posting. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our application.

2. Information We Collect

Our application may collect the following information:

  • Facebook Page access tokens (for posting content to your authorized pages)
  • Page ID and basic page information
  • Images and captions submitted for posting

3. How We Use Information

We use the collected information solely for:

  • Publishing content (images and text) to your authorized Facebook Page
  • Managing and automating your page posting workflow

4. Data Storage

All data is stored locally on the user's own device. We do not transmit or store your data on any external servers. Access tokens are stored in local environment files and are never shared with third parties.

5. Third-Party Access

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your information to third parties. The only external communication is with the Facebook Graph API for the purpose of publishing content to your page.

6. Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your data. Access tokens are stored in environment variables and are not hardcoded or exposed publicly.

7. Data Retention

Data is retained only on your local device for as long as you use the application. You can delete all stored data at any time by removing the application files.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the data we process
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Revoke Facebook permissions at any time through your Facebook settings

9. Children's Privacy

Our application is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
